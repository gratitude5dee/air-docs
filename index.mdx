Airv is your personal AI agent — not a shared chatbot, but a dedicated assistant that has its own phone number, its own `@wzrd.tech` email address, and its own persistent cloud computer running just for you. Everything your agent does, learns, and builds stays on your machine, private and isolated from everyone else.

## What makes Airv different

Most AI assistants are shared infrastructure — your conversation history sits next to thousands of other users, and the agent forgets everything when the session ends. Airv works the other way around.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Your own number" icon="phone">
    A dedicated iMessage line bound to you. Text it like a person in your contacts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your own inbox" icon="envelope">
    An `@wzrd.tech` email address your agent reads, drafts, and manages on your behalf.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your own computer" icon="desktop">
    A persistent Ubuntu VM where your agent lives — files, memory, and tools survive every conversation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Start exploring

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Your operator sends you an invite link. Tap it in Messages and send the pre-filled text to claim your line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity">
    Complete a quick phone OTP — this ties a crypto wallet to your account and makes your number the root of trust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your username">
    Choose a username to activate your `<username>@wzrd.tech` email address and shareable contact card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Text your new number, open the web app, or email your agent — all three share the same conversation context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Airv is currently in private beta. You need an invite from an operator to get started. See [Quick Start](/quickstart) for the full onboarding flow.
</Note>
