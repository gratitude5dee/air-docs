Every Airv account comes with a non-custodial crypto wallet, created automatically when you verify your phone number during onboarding. Your wallet is your agent's financial instrument — it can interact with EVM and Solana chains on your behalf, hold tokens, and execute on-chain transactions. You stay in control: nothing leaves your wallet without your explicit approval.

## How your wallet is created

Your wallet is provisioned at the moment you complete phone OTP verification during onboarding, powered by **thirdweb**'s infrastructure. Your phone number is the root of trust — it's what ties the wallet to you, and it's what you use to recover access if you ever need to.

Airv never holds your private keys. The wallet lives in thirdweb's non-custodial infrastructure, and your VM is granted only a **scoped credential** — one that can request a signature on your behalf, but cannot produce one unilaterally.

## Approval gates

Your agent can propose on-chain transactions, but it cannot execute them without your sign-off. Every transaction your agent initiates appears in **Needs You** — you see the amount, destination address, chain, and the reason your agent is requesting it before anything happens.

This applies to every on-chain action: transfers, contract interactions, swaps, and any other wallet operation.

<Info>
  Your agent never has unilateral access to your funds. The scoped credential your VM holds can queue a transaction for your review, but the signature that executes it requires your explicit approval.
</Info>

## The Wallet tab

Open the **Wallet** section in the web app to access everything in one place:

- Your **current balance** and token breakdown by chain
- Your **QR funding code** — display it to receive funds from any external wallet or exchange
- Your **full transaction history**, filterable by chain and date

<Warning>
  Do not share your wallet QR code publicly. It exposes your wallet address, which anyone can use to send you funds — but also allows others to track your on-chain activity. Share it only with people or services you trust.
</Warning>

## Security model

Airv's wallet infrastructure is built around a few key principles:

- **Non-custodial**: Airv never holds your private keys. Recovery is always possible through your verified phone number via thirdweb.
- **Scoped VM access**: your VM receives only a credential that can request a signature — not one that can produce one. Your agent must route every transaction through the approval flow.
- **No raw keys in your environment**: your private key is never written to your VM's filesystem, logged, or transmitted through Airv's servers.
- **Phone-based recovery**: if you lose access, thirdweb's phone-based recovery flow re-verifies your identity before restoring wallet access.
