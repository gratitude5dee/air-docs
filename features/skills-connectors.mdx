Your agent ships with a solid foundation, but its real power comes from what you add to it. Skills let you install new capability packages — image generation, browser automation, code execution, and more — while Connectors wire up the external services your agent needs to act on your behalf. Together, they turn your agent from a capable assistant into a fully integrated extension of your workflow.

## Skills

A skill is a self-contained package that teaches your agent how to do something new. Installing a skill places it on your VM and makes it available to your agent at the start of every subsequent session.

**Available skills include:**

- **Image generation** — create images from text prompts or reference photos
- **Video generation** — produce short video clips from descriptions or existing media
- **Music creation** — compose and arrange audio tracks
- **Code execution** — run scripts in isolated environments and return results
- **Browser automation** — control a headless browser to scrape, fill forms, or test web apps

### Installing a skill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Skills tab">
    In the web app, navigate to the **Skills** tab in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find what you need">
    Use the search bar or browse by category to find the skill you want to install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    Click **Install** on the skill card. The skill is added to your VM and activated for your agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle on or off">
    Use the toggle on any installed skill to enable or disable it. Changes take effect at the start of your agent's next session — no restart required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Skills are installed persistently on your VM. You won't be charged for re-installation on reconnect, and your agent won't spend time setting up tools it already has.
</Info>

## Connectors

Connectors give your agent access to third-party services through OAuth — the same secure authorization flow you use when you "Sign in with Google" on a website. The integration is powered by **Composio**, which handles token storage and provides your agent with a scoped MCP (Model Context Protocol) endpoint.

**Services you can connect include:**

- Gmail
- GitHub
- Google Calendar
- Slack
- Linear
- Notion
- And many more from the Composio catalog

### Connecting a service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Connectors tab">
    In the web app, navigate to the **Connectors** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a service">
    Browse or search for the service you want to connect, then click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    A secure mini-app opens the service's OAuth screen. Sign in and grant the requested permissions. You're redirected back to Airv when complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your agent is ready">
    The connected account is stored by Composio and your agent gains access to that service's tools automatically in every subsequent session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Revoking a connector

To disconnect a service, open the **Connectors** tab, find the connected account, and click **Disconnect**. Your agent immediately loses access to that service's tools.

### Security model

<Note>
  Airv never stores your OAuth tokens in its database, and they never touch your VM. Composio holds the tokens on your behalf and exposes only a **per-user MCP endpoint** to your agent — a scoped interface that lets your agent call tools without ever seeing the raw credential. You can revoke access at any time from the Connectors tab.
</Note>

<Note>
  Once you connect Gmail, your agent can read your inbox and take action on emails — all within a single conversation turn, without switching apps.
</Note>
