Your agent can run your entire content calendar — drafting posts, generating creatives, scheduling slots, and publishing to your connected platforms — without you having to touch a scheduler or ad dashboard. Every piece of content your agent proposes goes through your approval before it goes live. You set the strategy; your agent handles the execution.

## Content slots

A **slot** is a single scheduled post: one platform, one connected account, one piece of content, and one publish time. Your content calendar is made up of slots.

Your agent proposes slots based on:
- Your **brand kit** — palette, typography, tone of voice, imagery rules, and any claims restrictions
- **Connected calendar sources** — e-commerce product launches, touring schedules, or any other feed you've connected
- Your **direct instructions** — tell your agent what to post and when, and it builds the slot for you

### Slot lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Proposed">
    Your agent creates a slot and it appears in **Needs You** for your review. No content is scheduled until you approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approved and scheduled">
    You approve the slot. It moves to the scheduled queue and will auto-publish at the appointed time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Published">
    At the scheduled time, your agent publishes the content to the platform using your connected account. The slot moves to the published history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can **reschedule** or **cancel** any slot at any time before it publishes by finding it in the calendar view and editing the time or deleting the slot.

<Note>
  Your agent never publishes content without your explicit approval. Every proposed slot sits in **Needs You** until you review it — nothing goes live automatically.
</Note>

## Brand kit

Your agent uses your brand kit to keep every piece of generated content on-brand. The brand kit includes your color palette, typography choices, tone of voice, imagery guidelines, and any claims that are approved or restricted for your accounts.

Set up and update your brand guidelines in [Brand Kit](/settings/brand-kit). The more detail you provide, the more accurately your agent can generate content that looks and sounds like you.

## Ad campaigns

Your agent can manage paid advertising across Meta and OpenAI Ads — but every action that touches your budget requires your sign-off.

**What your agent can propose:**
- Create a new campaign
- Update an existing campaign's budget or status
- Create or modify an ad group
- Upload new ad creatives

**Approval:** All ad writes appear in **Needs You** before execution. Your agent never submits changes to an ad platform without your approval.

### Spend ceiling

Set a **monthly spend cap** per ad account in the account settings. Your agent will not propose or execute any action that would push your account past the cap in the current billing month. This prevents runaway ad spend even if your agent misinterprets a budget instruction.

### Analytics

View campaign performance in the **Ads** tab of the web app. Metrics include impressions, clicks, spend, and conversions, broken down by campaign and ad group.

## Connecting your platforms

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect social accounts">
    Open the **Connectors** tab and connect Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube via OAuth. Each platform walks you through its own authorization flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect ad accounts">
    For Meta Ads and OpenAI Ads, go to **Settings → Ad Accounts** and follow the setup flow for each platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your spend ceiling">
    In **Settings → Ad Accounts**, set a monthly spend cap for each connected ad account before giving your agent any ad-related instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brief your agent">
    Tell your agent your publishing goals — posting cadence, upcoming campaigns, content themes — and it will start proposing slots for your review.
  </Step>
</Steps>
