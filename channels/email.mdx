Your agent comes with its own email inbox on the `@wzrd.tech` domain. Once you pick a username, your agent can read incoming messages, draft threaded replies, and surface anything that needs your attention — all without ever sending a single email without your say-so.

## Activating Your Email Address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** in the Airv web app at [air.wzrd.tech](https://air.wzrd.tech).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a username">
    Enter your preferred username. Your agent's address immediately becomes `<username>@wzrd.tech`. The address is live as soon as you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Add it to your email signature, contact card, or anywhere else you'd share an address. Anyone who emails it goes straight to your agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can rename your username later, but only once every 30 days — so plan ahead before you publish the address widely.
</Note>

## What Your Agent Does with Email

Your agent is a full participant in your inbox, not just a notification relay.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Read and summarize" icon="envelope-open-text">
    Your agent reads every incoming message and gives you a summary in the **Needs You** queue or in chat, so you always know what arrived without having to open every thread.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Draft replies" icon="pen-to-square">
    Ask your agent to reply to a thread and it drafts a response with proper threading and quoted history stripped out — so the model focuses on what actually matters, not a wall of forwarded text.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Approval queue" icon="circle-check">
    Every outbound draft lands in **Needs You** before it goes anywhere. You review, edit if needed, and approve. Only then does it send.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CC on threads" icon="reply-all">
    Forward any email thread to your agent's address and it joins the conversation as a CC'd participant, reading the full context and drafting from there.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The Draft-Approval Guarantee

Your agent **never sends email on its own.** This is a structural guarantee, not just a setting. The email key your agent holds can create drafts but does not have permission to send. Every outbound message must be explicitly approved by you in the **Needs You** queue before it leaves your inbox.

<Warning>
  No configuration change, shortcut, or skill can bypass this. If you see an email sent from your address that you didn't approve, contact support immediately.
</Warning>

## Emailing Your Agent Directly

Any email sent to `<username>@wzrd.tech` lands in your agent's inbox. Replies thread correctly in both Gmail and Apple Mail — your agent sees the full conversation context when it drafts a response.

<Tip>
  For long-running projects, CC your agent's address on the original outbound email so it captures the entire thread from the start, not just the replies it receives later.
</Tip>

## Sharing Your Agent's Address

Your agent's email address works as a public-facing contact point. Include it in your email signature, on your website, or anywhere you'd put a contact email. Anyone who writes to it becomes a **Tier 2 sender** — your agent reads their message and surfaces it, but takes no action without your approval first.

## Renamed Addresses Never Break

If you change your username, the old `@wzrd.tech` address keeps routing to you permanently. Contacts who had your previous address will never get a bounce. You don't need to reach out to people to update their records.

<Info>
  Old addresses accumulate but they all point to the same inbox. You can think of them as permanent aliases.
</Info>
