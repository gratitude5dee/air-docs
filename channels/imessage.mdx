Your Airv agent has its own iMessage number — dedicated to you, not shared with anyone else. Once you save the contact card your agent sends, you can text it exactly like you'd text a person: send a quick thought, a long question, a photo, or a voice note, and your agent picks it up and responds.

## Finding Your Agent's Number

Your agent's iMessage number was included in your invite email and on the contact card your agent sends automatically after your first exchange. Save that contact card right away — once your agent is a saved contact in Messages, the "Report Junk" banner disappears permanently and messages display correctly with your agent's name and photo.

<Note>
  If you haven't received a contact card yet, send your agent any message and it will reply with one automatically.
</Note>

## How Replies Work

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Streaming replies" icon="signal-stream">
    Your agent's reply appears in the message bubble immediately and grows in place as it thinks. You watch the answer form — you're not waiting for a finished essay to land all at once.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Typing indicator" icon="ellipsis">
    A typing indicator appears while your agent is actively working on a response, so you always know it received your message and is on it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Burst debounce" icon="messages">
    Send multiple messages in quick succession and your agent waits for you to finish before it starts replying. It addresses your actual question, not just the first fragment you sent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cold-start warm-up" icon="clock">
    If your VM was asleep, expect the typing indicator to show for about 30 seconds on your first message of the session. Once the VM is running, all subsequent messages in that session respond quickly.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Sending Attachments

You can send photos and files directly through iMessage. Your agent retrieves the attachment, processes it, and incorporates it into its response — whether that's reading a PDF, describing an image, or extracting data from a spreadsheet.

<Tip>
  For large files or complex documents, follow up with a specific question after sending the attachment so your agent knows exactly what you need from it.
</Tip>

## Voice Messages

Record a voice note in Messages the same way you would with anyone else. Your agent transcribes the audio and responds to what you said. This is useful when you're on the go and typing is inconvenient.

## Interactive App Cards

Some responses arrive as interactive cards inside the Messages app rather than plain text bubbles. Cards update in place — tapping a button doesn't create a new message bubble, it updates the existing card.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Kanban board card" icon="table-columns">
    View and update a task board directly in Messages. Tap a card to move it between columns without opening a browser.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Approval card" icon="circle-check">
    When your agent needs a decision from you — like approving a drafted email or confirming a scheduled action — it surfaces an approval card you can accept or reject with one tap.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Trust Tiers and Sharing Your Number

Your agent treats senders differently depending on who they are.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tier 0 — You (Owner)">
    Messages from your own phone number are fully trusted. Your agent acts on your instructions immediately without any approval gate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tier 2 — Everyone else">
    If you share your agent's iMessage number with other people — for example, by passing it along as a contact — those people can text your agent too. Their messages arrive as Tier 2 (unknown sender) and require your explicit approval before your agent acts on any request from them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Be thoughtful about who you share your agent's number with. Anyone with the number can send it messages, but they cannot make your agent act without your approval.
</Warning>
