Your agent doesn't live in a single app. You can reach it wherever you already spend your time — in iMessage, in your email client, or in the Airv web app at [air.wzrd.tech](https://air.wzrd.tech). No matter which channel you use, you're talking to the same agent with the same memory and the same context. Switch from iMessage to email mid-conversation and your agent won't miss a beat.

## Three Channels, One Conversation

All three channels connect to the same underlying Hermes session (`air-main`), so your conversation history and memory are always consistent regardless of how you reach out.

  <Card title="iMessage" icon="message">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">

<Note>
  Email threads are scoped slightly differently: each distinct email thread gets its own session context, separate from your main `air-main` session. This keeps long email chains self-contained and prevents cross-thread confusion.
</Note>

## Channel-Specific Features

While all channels share the same agent and memory, each one has features tailored to how that medium works.

### iMessage

iMessage is the most immediate way to reach your agent. It's designed for quick back-and-forth and for catching decisions on the go.

- **Streaming replies** — text grows in place as your agent composes its response, just like a fast typist
- **App cards** — rich interactive cards that let you approve, dismiss, or act on decisions without leaving Messages
- **Typing indicator** — you see the familiar three-dot indicator while your agent is working
- **Contact card** — your agent's number is pre-configured as a contact so it's recognizable in your list
- **Voice messages** — send a voice memo and your agent transcribes and responds to it automatically

### Email

Email is best for longer requests, formal drafts, and anything you want a paper trail for.

- **Draft approval flow** — your agent writes emails but never sends them without your explicit approval. Every outbound draft appears in your [Needs You](/concepts/needs-you) queue first.
- **Threading** — your agent replies in-thread, maintaining proper email headers so conversations stay organized in your client
- **Reply extraction** — quoted history from prior replies is stripped before the message reaches the model, preventing context bloat and keeping responses accurate

### Web Chat

The web app is the most capable surface — use it for complex tasks, creative work, and managing your agent's settings.

- **Streaming SSE** — responses stream token by token directly in the browser
- **Voice transcription input** — click the microphone and speak your request; it transcribes before sending
- **Creative commands** — slash commands unlock generative capabilities:

| Command | What It Does |
|---------|--------------|
| `/imagine` | Generates an image from your description |
| `/animate` | Creates a short animation from a prompt or uploaded image |
| `/zap` | Triggers a connected automation or workflow |

## How Routing Works

Every inbound message — regardless of channel — passes through the same routing layer before reaching your agent. This layer resolves your identity, applies [trust tier](/concepts/trust-tiers) rules, and delivers the message to your VM. New channels like WhatsApp plug into this same routing system, so they inherit the same session continuity and trust model automatically.

<Tip>
  You don't need to configure routing. When a new channel is added to Airv, it's available to you without any setup — your agent recognizes messages from it the same way it recognizes messages from existing channels.
</Tip>
