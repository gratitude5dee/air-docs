Your username is the foundation of your agent's identity. Once you set it, your agent gets a dedicated email address and a public contact card that anyone can use to reach you — no account required on their end.

## Setting Your Username

Your username must follow a few rules before it can be claimed:

- **Length**: 2–24 characters
- **Characters**: lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only (`[a-z0-9_]`)
- **Reserved words**: some words are reserved system-wide and cannot be claimed

As soon as you save a valid username, two things activate instantly:

- **Email address**: `<username>@wzrd.tech` becomes your agent's live inbox
- **Public contact card**: your card goes live at `air.wzrd.tech/@<username>`

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Profile** in the Airv web app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your username">
    Type your desired username into the **Username** field. The app validates the format in real time and tells you immediately if the name is taken or reserved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. Your email address and contact card activate within seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing Your Username

You can rename yourself at any time — but changes are rate-limited to protect anyone who already has your contact details saved.

<Warning>
  You can only change your username **once every 30 days**. Plan ahead before you rename, especially if you've shared your email address publicly.
</Warning>

When you rename, here's what happens to your old identity:

- **Old email routes permanently**: emails sent to your previous `@wzrd.tech` address continue to arrive in your inbox forever. Nothing is lost, and senders don't receive a bounce.
- **iMessage number is unchanged**: your agent's iMessage number is tied to your account, not your username. Renaming has no effect on it.

<Warning>
  Even though your old email address keeps working, anyone who sees it in a signature, bio, or directory will still see the outdated address. After renaming, update any place you've shared your email so new contacts use the current one.
</Warning>

## Your Public Contact Card

Your contact card is a lightweight public page that lives at `air.wzrd.tech/@<username>`. Anyone can open it without logging in to Airv.

The card displays:

- Your agent's name and photo
- Your iMessage number
- Your `@wzrd.tech` email address

<Note>
  The contact card shows only contact information. No tasks, messages, files, or other private data are ever visible on the public card.
</Note>

You can share the card URL directly, or download the **vCard** from the card page to send to contacts who prefer to save you to their address book the traditional way.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Share the URL" icon="link">
    Copy `air.wzrd.tech/@<username>` and paste it anywhere — email signatures, social bios, Linktree, Notion pages, or a plain text message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Download the vCard" icon="address-card">
    Click **Download vCard** on your contact card page to get a `.vcf` file your contacts can import into Apple Contacts, Google Contacts, or Outlook.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
