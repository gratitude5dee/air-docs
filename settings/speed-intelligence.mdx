Your agent's Speed & Intelligence setting controls the trade-off between how quickly it responds and how deeply it reasons through a problem. You pick a tier; Airv handles the rest. You never need to name a model or tweak a parameter.

## The Three Tiers

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="⚡ Fast" icon="bolt">
    **Quickest responses.** Best for conversational back-and-forth, simple lookups, quick clarifying questions, and any task where getting an answer in seconds matters more than exhaustive analysis.
  </Card>
  <Card title="⚖️ Balanced" icon="scale-balanced">
    **A reliable middle ground.** Handles most everyday work well — coding help, email drafts, research summaries, scheduling, and general Q&A — without the wait time of Deep.
  </Card>
  <Card title="🔬 Deep" icon="brain">
    **Deepest reasoning available.** Use this for complex multi-step planning, long-form writing, critical analysis, debugging intricate code, or any task where accuracy and thoroughness outweigh speed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Change Your Tier

You can switch tiers at any point — no restart, no interruption to ongoing tasks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Speed & Intelligence** in the Airv web app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a tier">
    Click **Fast**, **Balanced**, or **Deep**. The currently active tier is highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your change takes effect immediately">
    The new tier applies starting with the very next message your agent processes. Any response already in progress finishes on the tier that was active when it started.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Changes Under the Hood

Each tier maps to a specific AI model running on Airv's infrastructure. You interact only with the tier name — Airv manages model selection, routing, and upgrades behind the scenes.

<Info>
  When Airv upgrades the underlying model for a tier, you automatically benefit without changing any setting. Your tier stays the same; the capability improves.
</Info>

This means you never need to track model version numbers or migration guides. If your agent gets smarter on Balanced next month, that's Airv's doing, not a config change on your end.

## Compute Spend

Tiers are not equally weighted against your monthly spend cap.

- **Fast** uses the least compute per turn.
- **Balanced** uses a moderate amount.
- **Deep** uses the most compute per turn — notably more than Fast or Balanced.

<Warning>
  If you have a monthly spend cap set on your account, running your agent on **Deep** for extended sessions will count against it faster. Consider switching back to Balanced or Fast for lighter tasks to preserve your budget for work that genuinely benefits from deeper reasoning.
</Warning>

## Which Tier Should You Start With?

<Note>
  Start with **Balanced**. It handles the vast majority of tasks well and gives you a reliable baseline for judging when you actually need to go faster or deeper.
</Note>

A practical guide:

| Situation | Recommended tier |
|---|---|
| Quick Q&A, casual chat, simple lookups | Fast |
| Email drafts, coding help, research summaries | Balanced |
| Complex planning, long-form writing, critical analysis | Deep |
| Debugging a tricky bug with many moving parts | Deep |
| Scheduling and reminders | Fast |
| Reviewing a contract or legal document | Deep |

Switch freely — there's no penalty for changing tiers multiple times in a session.
