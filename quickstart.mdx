Airv is currently in private beta. Before you can do anything, an operator must provision your account and send you a personal invite link. Once you have that link, the entire setup takes under two minutes — your agent walks you through every step over iMessage.

## How the Invite Works

Your operator sends you an `sms:` deep link out-of-band — through a text, email, or DM. When you tap it, your iPhone's Messages app opens pre-addressed to your new, dedicated Airv number. All you have to do is hit **Send**.

That first message is intentional. Airv is **inbound-first**: your agent never texts you cold on a new line. It always waits for you to initiate contact. This keeps your number out of spam filters and puts you in control of when the conversation starts.

## Onboarding Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap your invite link and send the first message">
    Open the `sms:` link your operator sent you. Messages launches with your agent's dedicated number already in the To field. Tap **Send** — even a blank message works. This activates your account and your agent responds within a few seconds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your phone number">
    Your agent sends a one-time passcode (OTP) to your phone via [thirdweb](https://thirdweb.com). Enter the code your agent asks for. This step proves you own the number and automatically creates a self-custodied crypto wallet tied to your account — no seed phrase setup required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your username">
    Your agent prompts you to pick a username. This becomes your permanent identity across Airv and activates your personal email address: `<username>@wzrd.tech`.

    Usernames must follow these rules:
    - **2–24 characters** long
    - **Lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only** — no spaces or special characters
    - Must be unique across all Airv users

    <Note>
      You can rename your username later, but only **once every 30 days**. Your old `@wzrd.tech` address keeps working after a rename so existing senders aren't broken — but pick something you like.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your agent's contact card">
    Once your username is set, your agent sends you a contact card. **Save it to your contacts.** This ensures your agent shows up as a recognized sender in Messages rather than an unknown number that iOS might route to the Junk or Unknown Senders folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Three Ways to Reach Your Agent

After onboarding you can talk to your agent from anywhere. All three surfaces share the exact same conversation context — start a task on iMessage and check the result in the web app, or reply to an email your agent sent you and the thread continues seamlessly.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="iMessage" icon="message">
    Text your agent at its dedicated number anytime. Works with all iMessage features — tapbacks, replies, and rich link previews.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Web App" icon="browser">
    Visit [air.wzrd.tech](https://air.wzrd.tech) for a full chat interface. Useful on desktop or when you want a larger screen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Send a message to `<username>@wzrd.tech`. Your agent reads and replies to email just like any other message — great for forwarding threads or attachments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Not sure which surface to use? Start with iMessage — it's the fastest path and the one your agent is most optimized for.
</Tip>
