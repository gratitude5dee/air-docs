Airv is a personal AI agent that belongs entirely to you. Unlike shared AI assistants, every Airv account runs its own isolated agent: your own iMessage number, your own `@wzrd.tech` email address, and your own persistent cloud computer that stores your agent's memory, files, and skills. Nothing is shared with other users.

## How it's different

Most AI assistants are multitenant services — your history lives alongside thousands of other users, and the agent resets after each session. Airv inverts this model:

- **Isolated by design**: your agent runs in its own VM. No other user's data is on your machine, and your machine holds no credentials that could affect anyone else.
- **Persistent memory**: everything your agent learns, saves, and builds stays on your private cloud computer. There's no session expiry and no amnesia.
- **Approval before action**: your agent never sends an email or posts to social media without your explicit sign-off. Every consequential action passes through the [Needs You](/concepts/needs-you) queue.
- **You control the intelligence tier**: choose Fast, Balanced, or Deep in Settings. Your agent uses the right AI model for the task without you ever configuring a model name.
